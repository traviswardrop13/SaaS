Good to know before you assign it.

Sona is the worksheet that listens. It supports your therapy; it doesn't diagnose, evaluate or set goals.

Read it as practice volume

The check asks whether a sound has the acoustic shape of the target. That's narrower than your ear. Treat pass rate as a rough signal, not a score.

Tuned to General American English

Dialectal or bilingual speech, like AAVE or Spanish-influenced English, may be marked as misses. Use your clinical judgment.

You decide readiness

Assign targets the child is developmentally ready for. Vocalic R, for example, isn't typically expected until about age 7.
