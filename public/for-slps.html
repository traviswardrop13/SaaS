What to know before you assign it

✓
It supplements your therapy — it doesn't replace it

Sona is a home-practice tool for carryover. It doesn't diagnose, evaluate, or set goals — you do. Think of it as the worksheet that listens.

🎧
Where the scoring is strong (and where it isn't)

Word, phrase, and sentence practice gets real phoneme-level scoring. Isolation and syllable steps are imitation warm-ups — encouraging, but not a graded substitute for your ear. It will never tell a child a wrong sound was perfect, and it won't reward noise.

🗣️
Accent & dialect

Scoring is tuned to General American English. It may flag features of regional, dialectal, or bilingual speech (e.g. AAVE or Spanish-influenced English) as errors — please use your clinical judgment when choosing targets.

📅
Pick developmentally-appropriate targets

Assign sounds the child is ready for — e.g. vocalic R isn't typically expected until ~age 7. Sona practices the targets you set; it doesn't decide readiness.

🔒
How a child's audio is handled

First name only. A recording is sent to our pronunciation-scoring provider to score that moment; it is not used to train models or kept beyond scoring. Saved practice clips stay on the child's own device. A parent sets up the child and consents.
