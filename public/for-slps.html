Home practice is the hard part.

You get a few minutes with each child a week. The reps in between are up to home.

Worksheets come back blank

Or they don’t come back at all.

Parents aren’t sure how to help

So practice gets skipped, or turns into a chore.

Sona turns practice into games. Kids say their sound to play, Sona checks each try on the phone, and you see who practiced.

What you can do with Sona.

  • Pick Sona games for each session

    Choose a sound and an age. Sona picks three of its games, and you play them right on your device, set to that child’s sound.

  • Share the app with clients

    Email a family a login for the Sona app, or send a link, so parents can set up Sona on their own device.

  • See who practiced at home

    Days practiced and words said out loud for every child, with a progress note you can copy.

  • Send homework home

    Pick the sound and how many words a day. It’s waiting in the family’s Sona app.

Session games sends Anthropic, our AI provider, the sound, word position, age band and session length you choose and the few words you type about what the child loves, to pick the games, and Sona doesn't keep them; leave out children's names. A game you play from Session games runs on your device and saves nothing to a family's Sona. Analytics (PostHog) on grown-up pages, and anonymous counts our server passes on from the app, and a Meta ad pixel on this sign-up page so we can tell which of our ads work — neither ever runs where a child practices, and neither is sent a child's name.
