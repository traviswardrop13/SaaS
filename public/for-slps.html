What you can do with Sona.

  • Pick Sona games for each session

    Choose a sound and an age. Sona picks three of its games, and you play them right on your device, set to that child’s sound.

  • Share the app with clients

    Email a family a login for the Sona app, or send a link, so parents can set up Sona on their own device.

  • See who practiced at home

    Days practiced and words said out loud for every child, with a progress note you can copy.

  • Send homework home

    Pick the sound and how many words a day. It’s waiting in the family’s Sona app.

If you start from this page: your email, kept by Sona and on our email list at Resend so Rachel's tips can reach you. Your account: email, name, clinic and code. Your card, when you start a free trial: held by Stripe, which runs the checkout and the billing. Sona never sees the card number. For each consenting family: the child's first name and age, target sounds, practice days and counts, pass rate per sound, your assignments, and when the parent consented. Hosted on Vercel, stored in Upstash, emails and the email list via Resend. If you type a parent's email when you add a child, we hand it to Resend to deliver that one email with the link. Sona's own server keeps no copy: not stored, not logged. Add a family emails the parent their Sona login too, so that email holds the password; Sona's server keeps only a scrambled copy of it. Session games sends Anthropic, our AI provider, the sound, word position, age band and session length you choose and the few words you type about what the child loves, to pick the games, and Sona doesn't keep them; leave out children's names. A game you play from Session games runs on your device and saves nothing to a family's Sona. Analytics (PostHog) on grown-up pages, and anonymous counts our server passes on from the app, and a Meta ad pixel on this sign-up page so we can tell which of our ads work — neither ever runs where a child practices, and neither is sent a child's name. No ads in the app, no data sales, no profiling.
