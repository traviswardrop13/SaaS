Accuracy by sound

How clearly each sound is coming along — and how it's trending with practice.

Reps are counted by loudness, right on this device. During games the mic listens in the moment for your child's voice — nothing is recorded and nothing is uploaded while they play.

Silence never counts. A rep only counts when your child actually speaks up, so the numbers on this page are real tries — not screen time.

One honest spot-check per round. Once each round, Sona listens for the actual sound. A miss simply becomes a warm try-again for your child — never a penalty, never a red mark.

No padding, ever. Mercy retries, offline rounds, and unsure checks are logged as nothing at all — never as progress. If this page says it happened, it happened.
