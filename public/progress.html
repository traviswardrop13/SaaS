Accuracy by sound

One sound check per practice burst, not a score for every try. Percentages appear after at least 10 tries over two days.

Tries come from sound checks during practice. The mic listens in the moment; only one clear try a day may be kept, on this phone, so you can listen back.

Silence never counts. A try needs a detected sound burst and a sound check the app can verify. Silence and unsure checks add nothing to these numbers. Noise, nearby voices or TV can be mistaken for practice. Choose a quiet spot.

One honest spot-check per round. Once each round, Sona listens for the actual sound. A miss simply becomes a warm try-again for your child — never a penalty, never a red mark.

Free play stays separate. Feed Echo, Bubble Pop, Peekaboo and game voice boosts are free play and do not add tries or accuracy here.

You choose what to share. Recordings stay on this phone unless you tap Share. Only pilot families who consented to progress sharing and have a connected speech-language pathologist account send practice summaries to their clinician. Those summaries contain no audio.
