About you

The key is what makes your links an entitlement rather than an honour system. Rotate it if a link gets posted somewhere it shouldn't — families who already joined keep syncing; new families need the new link.

Your caseload link

Any family can join with this one link. They pick the sound themselves, and you set homework once they appear. Adding a child from the Caseload page makes a per-child link with the homework already waiting.

The link opens Sona in the phone's browser and unlocks it — nothing to install, and it works on iPhone and Android. A family who would rather have an app icon can get the iPhone app free from the App Store and sign in with the same link; the link is what unlocks them, so send it either way.
