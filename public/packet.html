Three steps to home practice.

  • Circle the target

    Pick the sound and the place in the word the child is ready for. Each sound has its own page.

  • Send it home

    The parent guide shows families how to practice: one word on its own, a few minutes a day.

  • See the ticks

    Families tick a box each day they practice and bring the page to the next session.

Sona is an at-home speech practice tool. It is not a diagnosis, evaluation, or substitute for professional care.
