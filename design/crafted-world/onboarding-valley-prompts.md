# Setup screens in the valley, with Echo

Travis, 29 Sep 2026: "i like this art ... the onboarding pages should have
echo as main person." The target look is his two ChatGPT boards,
`references/06-chatgpt-valley-story.webp` and `references/07-chatgpt-app-sheet.webp`.

**Take from them:** the golden-hour mountain valley (flower meadow, mossy
rocks, pines, a lake, snowy peaks, big clouds, a soft rainbow), and the hero
standing big and full-body in the middle of the scene.

**Leave out:** the bunny and the robot (Echo leads setup, alone), the
"Continue with Apple / Google" screen (families don't sign in to Sona), and
all the words on the boards (our copy stays ours). Echo keeps Echo's own look,
since it is the app icon and the ads.

## How to make them

In ChatGPT, attach Echo's picture (`public/assets/crafted/echo-welcome.webp`),
paste the two blocks below once, then ask for each picture in turn. Send the
results back in the Claude chat; they get shrunk, swapped in, and checked on
a small and a big iPhone.

**Style block**

> Style: a warm, painterly 3D storybook look, like a Pixar short.
> Golden-hour sunlight from the right, soft glow, gentle depth of field. The
> world is a peaceful mountain valley: a flower meadow with small white daisies
> and a few blue flowers, mossy round boulders, tall green pine trees, a
> winding dirt path with a little wooden rail fence, a calm sparkling blue lake,
> snowy mountains far away, big soft white clouds in a clear blue sky, and a
> soft pastel rainbow. Bright, cozy and safe — nothing scary. No text, letters,
> numbers, logos, buttons or phone screens anywhere in the picture.

**Echo block**

> The character is Echo, from the picture I attached. Keep Echo exactly the
> same: a round teal-blue bird shaped like a ball, one coral-red and one yellow
> feather on top of the head, big white eyes with dark shiny pupils, a small
> orange beak, round pink cheeks, a pale blue belly, darker teal wings and two
> small orange feet. Smooth soft-clay look, not fluffy fur. Only Echo's pose
> and face change. Echo is the only character: no bunny, no robot, no other
> animals.

## The pictures

### 1. First screen — replaces the orange orchard

File: `public/assets/crafted/welcome-valley.webp` (1024 × 1536). Done 29 Sep 2026 — it replaced the orange orchard.

> Make a tall picture, 1024 × 1536. Echo stands on a mossy rock beside the
> lake, waving hello with one wing and smiling big. Words and a button go on
> top of this picture, so: keep the top third mostly open sky (clouds and the
> rainbow are fine, nothing busy); put Echo a little below the middle, inside
> the middle half of the width, because phones cut off the sides; make Echo
> about a third as wide as the picture; keep the bottom sixth simple, just soft
> grass and flowers.

### 2. Calm valley — behind the other setup screens (optional)

New file. Today those screens are plain cream.

> Make a tall picture, 1024 × 1536: the same valley with no characters. It sits
> behind a big white card, so keep it very calm and soft: mostly pale blue sky
> with soft clouds at the top, gentle blurry hills and pines low down, a soft
> meadow along the bottom edge. Low contrast, soft focus, nothing detailed in
> the middle.

### 3. Echo poses — the Echo who talks at the top of each screen

Today one picture of Echo is reused on every screen. For each pose below, use
this frame so they all line up:

> Square picture, 1024 × 1024, transparent background (if you can't, plain
> white). Echo alone, full body, centred, about 80% of the picture's height, a
> soft shadow under the feet. Same size and camera angle as the other poses.

| Screen | What Echo says there | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| Name and age | "Nice to meet you!" | Waving one wing, big happy smile. |
| Pick your sounds | "Let's pick our sounds!" | Tapping the beak with a wing tip, eyes looking up, curious smile. |
| Microphone | "Let's hear you!" | One wing cupped by the side of the head like listening, head tilted, eyes wide and excited. |
| Hand the phone over | "Hand the phone to Milo!" | Jumping with both wings up, feathers bouncing, huge smile, a few tiny sparkles. |
| Microphone is blocked | (grown-up help screen) | Small hopeful smile, one wing raised a little, like "try again?" Kind, never sad. |
